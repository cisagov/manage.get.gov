{% if is_federal %} {% if federal_type == 'executive' %}

Executive branch federal agencies

Domain requests from executive branch agencies must be authorized by the agency's CIO or the head of the agency.

See OMB Memorandum M-23-10 for more information.

{% elif federal_type == 'legislative' %}

Legislative branch federal agencies

U.S. Senate

Domain requests from the U.S. Senate must be authorized by the Senate CIO.

U.S. House of Representatives

Domain requests from the U.S. House of Representatives must be authorized by the House CIO,

Other legislative branch agencies or commissions

Domain requests from legislative branch agencies must come from the agency’s head or CIO.

Domain requests from legislative commissions must come from the head of the commission, or the head or CIO of the parent agency, if there is one.

{% elif federal_type == 'judicial' %}

Judicial branch federal agencies

Domain requests for judicial branch agencies, except the U.S. Supreme Court, must be authorized by the director or CIO of the Administrative Office (AO) of the United States Courts.

Domain requests from the U.S. Supreme Court must be authorized by the director of information technology for the U.S. Supreme Court.

{% endif %} {% elif organization_type == 'state_or_territory' %}

U.S. states and territories

For states and territories (executive branch), this might include:

  • State CIO
  • Department secretary
  • Senior technology officer
  • Chief election official

For states and territories (judicial and legislative branches), this might include:

  • Chief administrative officer
  • Director
  • Secretary
  • Senior technology officer
{% elif organization_type == 'tribal' %}

Tribal governments

Forfederally-recognized tribes , requests must be authorized by the tribal leader the Bureau of Indian Affairs recognizes.

For state-recognized tribes (that aren’t federally-recognized), requests must be authorized by the tribal leader the state recognizes.

{% elif organization_type == 'city' %}

Cities

For cities, this might include:

  • Mayor
  • Council president
  • City manager
  • Township / village supervisor
  • Select board chairperson
  • Chief
  • Senior technology officer
{% elif organization_type == 'county' %}

Counties

For counties, this might include:

  • Commission chair
  • County executive, judge, or mayor
  • County manager or administrator
  • Parish / borough president
  • Senior technology officer
  • Elections administrator

Other county-level offices (county clerk, sheriff, county auditor, comptroller) may qualify in some instances.

{% elif organization_type == 'school_district' %}

School districts

For school districts,

  • Board chair
  • Superintendent
  • Senior technology officer
{% elif organization_type == 'special_district' %}

Special districts

For special districts, this might include:

  • CEO
  • Chair
  • Executive director
  • Senior technology officer
{% elif organization_type == 'interstate' %}

Interstate organizations

For interstate organizations, this might include:

  • President
  • Director
  • Chair
  • Senior technology officer
  • One of the state's CIOs
